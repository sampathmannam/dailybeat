# DailyBeat 4.3.7 — reliability and release review

## Scope and isolation

This review covers the Android personal journal, capture and place-label flows, journey maps,
editing, review/search/export, privacy and recovery controls, reminders, and the protected release
pipeline. Tests use synthetic fixtures in the explicitly disposable QA package. Production history,
the installed production app, work profiles and unrelated connected devices are not modified.
No new coordinate-upload provider or cloud account is enabled.

## Corrections

- **Map startup and recovery:** ignore the SDK's initial unfitted camera when selecting local map
  coverage. Preserve one native host beneath failure overlays; retry does not detach and reuse a
  reset surface. Use TextureView rendering, guard destroyed callbacks and make lifecycle cleanup
  idempotent. Online failover is bounded and allowed only when online maps are already enabled.
- **Map cards:** preserve successfully loaded tiles within an overall eight-second budget and
  per-tile deadline. Recycle abandoned bitmaps on cancellation. Failed cards offer explicit retry
  and one retry on foreground return; successful cards do not poll or reload unnecessarily.
- **Place labels:** use saved/corrected names, then useful addresses, then clearly approximate
  coordinates. Repair only generated capture placeholders, not user-authored text. Approximate
  coordinates do not become a claimed venue or a recurring-place identity.
- **Metrics:** Days uses gap-adjusted capture intervals rather than first-to-last wall-clock time.
  A fixture spanning 11 hours 5 minutes correctly displays 10 tracked minutes in Today and Days.
  Weekly totals retain uncertainty from contributing estimated days. Review totals say Events.
- **Editing and accessibility:** consume parent insets, request keyboard resize, expand and scroll
  the note sheet, and make legacy restore one labelled checkbox-role toggle with a 48dp target.
- **Reminders:** offer review instead of falsely asserting a report is ready; preserve the reminder
  date, wait for the navigation graph on cold launch, consume handled intents, and reject malformed
  or out-of-range external input before route construction.
- **Privacy copy:** explicitly scope on-device processing to pattern analysis and acknowledge
  separately enabled Cloud AI reports. Existing consent and private-zone controls are unchanged.

Impeccable's hardening guidance informed bounded recovery, keyboard/large-text checks and accessible
controls. Existing colours and the Today/Days disclosure design are preserved.

## Local evidence before publication

- FOSS/store JVM suite: **634 tests, zero failures, errors or skips**.
- Repository/release-policy suite: **116 tests passed** after the version update.
- Fresh full lint rerun: **zero errors, 91 warnings and one hint**. Warnings are not hidden or
  converted into a zero-warning claim.
- Full offline/core instrumentation suite: **75 tests passed on physical Android 17**. The live
  authenticated-backup class is deliberately excluded locally and remains mandatory in CI.
- Real, hash-verified Tamil Nadu offline package: activation/deletion, streets and labels across
  nine locations, invalid-style recovery and repeated open/close/background/GC tests passed.
  The street-render test observed zero map HTTP resource requests; other subsystems were not
  claimed to be globally network-isolated by that test.
- Visually checked light/dark native maps and keyboard-open editing with synthetic fixtures.
- Accessibility semantics tested at 200% font/320dp; on-device keyboard tests verify the focused
  field and a saved note. Cold/warm reminder launch and activity recreation passed.
- QA process exit history showed no crash or ANR entries after the final full suite.

The final versioned commit must independently pass every protected check before publication:
standard and Google-free builds, instrumentation, live and Android-native encrypted backup round
trips, database row isolation, repository/release policy, secret/dependency/static analysis, and
reproducible store packaging. The publisher checks the permanent signing certificate and refuses
to overwrite existing releases. Published CI and release runs provide the final-commit evidence.

## Remaining limits

The native-map changes address unsafe lifecycle behavior and pass targeted regressions; they do
not prove the sole trigger of the previously observed rare native abort or eliminate every possible
crash. A day-long battery measurement, location/venue field study, spoken TalkBack assessment and
independent penetration test were not performed in this pass. Store acceptance is external.
MapLibre is retained; [alternative place-data research](../PLACE_DATA_OPTIONS.md) is documented,
but no unvalidated provider is shipped. GPS cannot identify every adjacent shop or prove attendance.
DailyBeat is a personal journal, not a forensic evidence system or an enterprise certification.
