# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

android

## Users

DailyBeat is primarily for an IPS officer using one Android phone throughout an active, interruption-heavy day. The user needs a trustworthy account of where they went, what happened, and what still needs attention without manually running a tracker or repeatedly typing notes.

## Product Purpose

DailyBeat passively captures movement and meaningful moments, turns each calendar day into a reviewable Beat, and produces an accurate personal and official diary. Success means the user can see whether the day was captured correctly, repair uncertainty quickly, complete the diary in under a minute, and retrieve useful patterns later without exposing sensitive location data.

## Positioning

Your whole day, remembered: a private, passive movement journal that becomes an accurate, correctable story every evening.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, purposeful. During capture the app should recede into the background; during review it should feel clear and reassuring; at completion it should provide restrained satisfaction rather than spectacle.

## Anti-references

- A generic warm-beige journal or administrative dashboard made from repeated rounded cards.
- A public social network, patrol leaderboard, employee-surveillance product, or opaque productivity score.
- A sports tracker that implies high-frequency precision when the underlying whole-day data is incomplete.
- A technical control panel that exposes model names, base URLs, coordinates, or diagnostics in the ordinary user path.
- Decorative motion, emoji placeholders, low-contrast accent text, or color used without a second signal.

## Design Principles

1. **Trust before delight.** Show recent-fix age, capture coverage, gaps, estimates, and repair actions before claiming the day is complete.
2. **The day is the product.** Treat the Beat—not a report, setting, or raw GPS point—as the primary object across Today, Days, Insights, and export.
3. **Review in under a minute.** Make inferred places and gaps easy to confirm, rename, merge, split, hide, or remove without procedural friction.
4. **Reward meaning, not movement.** Celebrate reviewed days, reflection, reliable capture, discoveries, and chosen routines; never reward excessive distance, speed, or longer work hours.
5. **Private by default.** Keep location and diary data local unless the user deliberately invokes a configured cloud or share action, and redact sensitive places before anything leaves the device.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast for text and controls, preserve meaning without color, support TalkBack, 200% font scaling, compact and expanded Android widths, dark theme, predictable system Back behavior, 48dp touch targets, and reduced-motion settings. Error states must explain what happened and provide a concrete recovery action without exposing technical exception text.
